# Day 1 Notes

## What is Salesforce?
Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM (Customer Relationship Management) platform used by companies to manage customers, sales, support, and business operations.

It helps businesses:
- Store customer information
- Track sales
- Automate workflows
- Improve customer support

---

## What is CRM?
CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.

CRM software helps companies:
- Manage customer data
- Track communication
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Increase sales efficiency

Example:
A company can track all customer interactions in one place using CRM.

---

## Why Companies Use Salesforce
- Centralized customer data
- Automation of repetitive tasks
- Better customer support
- Sales tracking
- Cloud accessibility
- Easy customization

---

# Salesforce Basic Concepts

## Object
An Object in Salesforce is similar to a database table.

Examples:
- Account
- Contact
- Lead
- Opportunity

Custom Example:
- Student
- Faculty

---

## Record
A Record is a single entry inside an object.

Example:
Inside Student Object:
- Praneeth
- Roll No: 101
- Department: CSE

This complete entry is one record.

---

## Field
A Field stores specific information.

Examples:
- Name
- Email
- Phone Number

---

# Standard Salesforce Objects

## Lead
A potential customer who has shown interest.

## Contact
An actual customer or person associated with a business.

## Account
Represents a company or organization.

## Opportunity
A potential sales deal.

---

# Salesforce Admin vs Developer

## Salesforce Admin
- Configures Salesforce
- Manages users
- Creates reports
- Uses clicks instead of code

## Salesforce Developer
- Writes Apex code
- Builds Lightning Web Components
- Creates custom applications
- Develops integrations

---

# Salesforce Data Model
Salesforce stores data using:
- Objects
- Records
- Fields
- Relationships

Objects are connected using relationships similar to databases.

---

# Real-World Application Idea
College Management System

## Objects Needed
- Student
- Faculty
- Course
- Attendance
- Department
- Fees

## Relationships
- One Department → Many Students
- One Faculty → Many Courses
- One Student → Many Attendance Records
